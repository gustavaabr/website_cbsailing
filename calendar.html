Where we are right now, and where we are racing next.

Right now

Copenhagen, Denmark

WhatPhysical training & technique at home
WhenUntil New Year

We are spending November and December training at home, focusing on physical training and technique. The everyday training is going great: we are having 4–5 sessions on the water every week and have focused a lot on technique and starts, getting many repetitions in.

We have also experimented quite a lot with our settings, testing different mast bend setups, and we are looking forward to testing it all against some fast boats.

Furthermore, we have started a year-long program with a Team Denmark sports psychologist, using the Lighthouse Model. The main goal is keeping our heads in the boat: blocking out external distractions like shifting weather, competitors or event logistics, so we can purely focus on the sailing.

Updated 15 November 2026

Training with the Danish national team

Cádiz, Spain
About the event

The Bay of Cádiz is one of Spain's classic winter training venues for Olympic classes, with strong Levante and Poniente winds and plenty of waves.

Our plan

We start the year in Cádiz with the Danish national team. For us it is a chance to line up against the best Danish boats every day, sharpen our boat handling in strong wind and get a lot of hours on the water early in the season.
