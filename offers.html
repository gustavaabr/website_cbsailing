Why we need support

Talent, not budgets

In Olympic sailing, the equipment decides whether talent can be turned into medals.

Over time the hull gets soft and heavy from moisture, and every extra kilo costs speed. The national team boats we race against get about two new 49ers a year.

Your support gives us the same starting point as them, so it is the sailors' talent that decides who goes to the Olympics, not just who has the most money.
